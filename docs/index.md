# Base Legal

**Every claim cites an inciso — and a machine checks it.**

Base Legal answers questions about Brazilian data protection law (the LGPD and
the resolutions of the ANPD's Board of Directors) with citations to the exact
article, paragraph and inciso. Every citation is checked verbatim against the
official text before it is shown; when nothing in the corpus supports an
answer, Base Legal refuses and shows the nearest provisions instead.

!!! warning "Not legal advice"
    Base Legal is a research tool with no affiliation to the ANPD or the
    Brazilian government. See the [legal notice](legal-notice.md).
